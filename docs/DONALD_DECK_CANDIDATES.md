# The Donald Deck — Six-Category Candidate Cards

Version 0.11 · 2 November 2026 · Editorial working draft, not publication-ready.

## Current category decision

Use **Pride, Greed, Lust, Envy, Wrath and Stupidity**. Greed absorbs Gluttony (excessive acquisition or consumption). Stupidity covers absurd claims, poor reasoning and lazy thinking; physical inactivity alone does not count. Sloth is not a separate attribute. This decision supersedes the seven-attribute assumption in the original requirements for this deck; the older requirements document remains a historical baseline pending a full revision.

All 25 spreadsheet rows are retained in original order, followed by the additional user-supplied quote as Donald #26 and a paired statement card as Donald #27, followed by the ego quote as Donald #28 and the campaign-profit quote as Donald #29 and the retaliation quote as Donald #30 and the marriage quote as Donald #31 and the Access Hollywood recording excerpt as Donald #32 and the Diana courtship quote as Donald #33 and the Japan bow quote as Donald #34. Incorrect quiz alternatives are removed. Correct-answer bold formatting did not survive the pasted table, so completions are provisional where not independently established. Quotation marks do not imply every draft has been verified.

## Scoring and overlap policy

- Scores are provisional satirical editorial judgments of each statement/context, not factual measurements of the speaker's character or intelligence. Higher wins; all six scores remain hidden until play.
- Scale: 1–20 negligible, 21–40 weak, 41–60 moderate, 61–80 strong, 81–100 very strong. These are raw intensity scores, not calibrated win probabilities.
- Each candidate has **at least three discussed overlaps**. Weak and negligible links are explicitly identified: three explanations do not mean three strong scores. A few quotes do not credibly support three meaningful overlaps and should be replaced before launch if that is a publication requirement.
- Aim for spread without inventing motives: insults are not automatically Envy; wealth is not automatically Greed; sexual references are not automatically evidence of attraction; political disagreement is not automatically Stupidity. Envy needs a comparison showing resentment of another's advantage.
- No mechanical conversion/max of the earlier Gluttony/Sloth scores was used. Every candidate was reconsidered for the six-category model.
- Card 19 is disputed/reported and remains HOLD. Its scores are hypothetical and excluded from balancing summaries. Other cards remain editorial candidates until original wording, attribution, context and rights checks are complete.
- Neutral fronts: Donald #01, etc. Explanations and category emphasis are reveal-only, to avoid hinting at the answer.
